# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes grocery product sales and outlet information using Microsoft Power BI. It aims to transform raw data into meaningful visual insights that help explore sales performance, product categories, outlet characteristics, and customer ratings.

The project uses a dataset containing product attributes, outlet details, item visibility, item weight, sales, and ratings.

## Objectives

- Analyze sales across different item categories.
- Compare sales across outlet types and location tiers.
- Explore the relationship between item fat content and sales.
- Examine outlet size and establishment year.
- Investigate item visibility and weight.
- Explore product ratings.
- Present key performance indicators through a Power BI dashboard.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — dashboard creation and reporting
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — measures and calculations, where applicable
- **Microsoft Excel** — source dataset


## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **8,523 records and 12 columns** describing grocery products and outlets.

The columns cover:
- Item identifiers and categories
- Item fat content
- Outlet identifiers and establishment year
- Outlet location tier, size, and type
- Item visibility and weight
- Sales and ratings

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance
Explore sales across product categories and outlet characteristics.

### 2. Product Analysis
Compare sales by item type and fat content.

### 3. Outlet Analysis
Investigate sales across outlet types, location tiers, sizes, and establishment years.

### 4. Product Characteristics
Explore item visibility and weight alongside sales.

### 5. Ratings
Examine the distribution of product ratings and compare ratings across available categories.

## Business Questions

- Which item categories contribute the most to sales?
- How do sales vary across outlet types?
- Which outlet location tiers generate the highest sales?
- How do sales differ by item fat content?
- How does outlet size relate to sales performance?
- How do sales vary across outlet establishment years?
- What patterns appear in item visibility, weight, and ratings?
